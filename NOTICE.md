# Public portfolio notice

This repository is an academic portfolio adaptation of a University of Washington MSIM practicum completed by a four-person team in collaboration with LumIntelligence and sponsor Peter Wang.

The public package is intentionally scoped:

- all donor profiles are synthetic test fixtures;
- phone and email fields are omitted;
- the 367 MB source graph and internal project-management materials are excluded;
- personal-relationship data is used as a sample to demonstrate information-quality challenges, not as the platform's sole purpose;
- only the completed pilot is reported as a retrieval result;
- the 50-source method comparison remains `NOT_RUN`;
- confidence-weighted edges are a proposed design, not an implemented or calibrated production feature.

No production, privacy, security, generated-answer quality, or cross-domain performance claim is made.
